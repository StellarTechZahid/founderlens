Questions people ask.

Does the research use live web data?
No. The agents write from their own knowledge, so every figure is an estimate and is labelled that way. Each research area ends with what to verify next, using customer interviews, price tests and official sources.
How is the score calculated?
The AI rates each criterion from 1 to 10. The software, not the AI, does the maths: ten criteria make a market score, and the two market scores make the overall score. That keeps the numbers consistent.
What happens to my answers?
They are stored on the server running this site, only to build your report. Your name, phone number and email are never sent to the AI.
Which AI runs this?
The two agents run as a workflow on DNotifier, using the OpenAI model connected to the DNotifier app (GPT-4o by default). Every run can be inspected step by step in the DNotifier dashboard.
