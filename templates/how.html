How it works

FoundersLens is a pipeline of two AI agents, built as a multi-agent workflow on DNotifier. Here is what happens between your answers and your PDFs.

Intake form

{{ question_count }} answers about your idea, saved on the server.

Evaluator agent

One AI call scores 20 criteria from 1 to 10 and explains each score.

Research agent

Four AI calls run in parallel and research 20 areas, flagging every estimate.

Score engine and PDFs

Code rebuilds the full structure, does the maths, and writes both PDFs.

Agent one scores

The evaluator reads your answers and rates your idea on ten Pakistan criteria and ten global criteria. It is told to be sceptical, to use the whole 1 to 10 range, and to score cautiously when your answers give no evidence.

Your name, phone number and email are not part of what it reads.

Agent two researches

The researcher takes your idea and the evaluator's verdict, then writes up 20 research areas. To keep every reply short enough to finish cleanly, the work is split into four parallel calls: Pakistan areas 1 to 5 and 6 to 10, global areas 1 to 5 and 6 to 10.

Each area gets findings, a short analysis, figures to verify and a confidence level. Each market also gets key risks and a 90-day validation plan.

The maths is done in code

AI is good at judgement and weak at arithmetic, so it only supplies the 1 to 10 scores and reasons. The software clamps every score to the valid range, averages ten criteria into a market score, averages the two markets into the overall score, and picks the verdict band.

8 and aboveStrong
6.5 to 7.9Promising
5 to 6.4Needs validation
3.5 to 4.9High risk
Below 3.5Weak

Every run can be traced

The pipeline is one DNotifier workflow with observability switched on. Each run gets an execution id, and the DNotifier dashboard shows every step with its input and output, which makes a surprising score easy to investigate.

What it cannot do

The agents do not browse the web. They write from their own knowledge, so figures are labelled as estimates and can be out of date. Scores are judgements for guidance, not verified market data.

Use the reports to decide what to test next, then check the important claims with customers, price tests and official sources.
